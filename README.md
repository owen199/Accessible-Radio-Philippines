Accessible Radio Philippines

Accessible Radio Philippines lets you listen to radio stations from all over the Philippines on your Android phone. It was designed first for blind and visually impaired listeners, so it works fully with TalkBack and other screen readers. It also has a clean and easy to read design, so everyone can enjoy it.

The app has more than 800 AM, FM, and online stations, organized into six tabs:

1. Favorites, where you keep the stations you love for quick access.
2. Luzon, including Metro Manila.
3. Visayas.
4. Mindanaw.
5. Online, for internet radio stations from the Philippines.
6. About, with information about the app.

In the Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanaw tabs, AM and FM stations are kept separate and grouped by region. This makes it easy to find a station from your home province or from anywhere else in the country.

Main features:

1. Fast playback. Stations start playing within moments. If a stream is down, the app automatically switches to a backup stream when one is available.
2. Live audio from official YouTube broadcasts for stations such as DZBB Super Radyo 594 and Radyo Pilipinas.
3. Complete volume controls. These include a volume slider, volume up, volume down, and mute buttons, a loudness boost for weak stations, a normalizer that evens out the volume between stations, six equalizer presets, balance, and mono sound.
4. Station options. Long press any station to add it to your favorites or copy its streaming link.
5. Search, so you can find any station by name.
6. Background playback. The radio keeps playing when the screen is off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
7. A sleep timer that turns off the radio after the time you choose.
8. Dark and light themes with high contrast colors.

Accessibility:

1. Every button and control has a clear label for screen readers.
2. Each section has headings, so you can move around the app quickly.
3. The app tells you what is happening, such as when a station is connecting, playing, or not available.
4. Buttons are large and easy to tap.
5. Bluetooth keyboard shortcuts let you control the app without touching the screen.

Station information comes from public radio directories and from the stations' own official streams. An internet connection through mobile data or WiFi is required.
